The House

Designed as an object. Composed as a scent.

ZARYA begins with form. Every fragrance concept is considered first as a physical presence: its mass, its edges, the way it holds a hard beam of light.

The scent follows the same discipline. Nothing ornamental, nothing borrowed. This is the early direction of a new house, shown honestly, before any bottle is on sale.
